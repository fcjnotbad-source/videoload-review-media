Privacy policy

Updated September 15, 2026

VideoLoad requires no account and includes no advertising or analytics/tracking SDK. We do not sell your personal data.

History, favorites, cookies, download records, playback progress, settings and privacy-lock credentials are stored locally. Imported videos are copied or referenced with your permission. The privacy lock does not encrypt files. Android backup or device transfer may restore app data, depending on system settings.

Browsing sends URLs, searches, cookies, your IP address and related request information directly to the websites, search engines and media hosts you use. Their privacy policies apply. Opening this policy or our support/download website also sends standard request data to its host. This policy page has no advertising or analytics scripts.

Internet access is used for browsing and downloads; notifications and foreground services show download progress; audio access controls playback; older Android versions need storage permission. File and folder imports use system authorization. Google Play handles Pro purchases under its own policy; purchase status is used to unlock features. Only the direct edition can request permission to install updates you choose.

You can clear history, cookies and other records in settings. Uninstalling removes private app data, but public downloads and referenced files may remain. Deleting a list entry does not guarantee the file was deleted if access is denied; use the system file manager to check and delete remaining files.

The app is not directed to children under 13. Save only content you own or may download; do not bypass DRM or download from YouTube. Policy revisions update the date above. For questions, use the support contact linked below.
