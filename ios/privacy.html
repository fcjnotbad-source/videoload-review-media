iPhone privacy

Privacy policy

For VideoLoad No Ads on iPhone, bundle ID com.fcj.videoload.ios. This policy describes the iPhone app, not an Android edition.

Updated 2026-11-10

1. The app and your data

The iPhone app requires no app account or sign-in. It includes no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK. The app does not upload your videos or a copy of your browsing history to a developer-operated server, and we do not sell your personal data.

Video processing, annotations, thumbnail generation and purchase-entitlement verification take place on your device. Apple and the websites or services you choose to use still process the requests described below.

2. Information stored on your device

Local app data includes browser tabs, page addresses and titles, history, bookmarks, cookies, download URLs and source-page information, download checkpoints, downloaded or imported media, thumbnails, annotations, playback positions and preferences. A download may retain request information needed to access the source, such as cookies, a browser user agent and a referring page.

If you enable the privacy lock, a salted, derived password verifier is kept in the device Keychain. Device authentication is handled by Apple; the app does not receive your Face ID or Touch ID biometric templates. The privacy lock controls access to the app interface; it does not encrypt your media files.

3. Browsing and downloads

Opening a website, submitting a search or downloading a file sends requests to the website, search engine or media host involved. Those services can receive your IP address, page or resource URL, browser information, relevant cookies and anything you submit to them. A new browser session may initially open Google. Third-party sites follow their own privacy policies.

Media discovery runs locally inside the web page and identifies playable resource addresses. Downloads contact their source hosts directly; we do not relay your videos through a developer server. Local HLS playback uses a private, temporary HTTP endpoint bound only to 127.0.0.1 on your device, with an unguessable session path and a local-file allowlist. It is not a public upload service.

4. Permissions, imports and sharing

Photo and file imports use Apple's system selectors and access only the items or folders you choose. Adding an exported video or GIF to Photos asks for add-only photo-library permission. Face ID or device authentication is optional and used to unlock the privacy lock. The app does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts or advertising-tracking access.

When you share, export or save a file elsewhere, the destination you select receives that copy. Your chosen file provider, iCloud storage or device-backup settings may store app data or exported files in your Apple account. Such storage is controlled by you and Apple or the selected provider, not a developer media cloud.

5. App Store purchases

Pro is a non-consumable, one-time App Store purchase. Apple processes payment, purchase history, refunds and restoration under its policies. The app reads verified StoreKit transaction status to unlock features; we do not receive your card details or operate a separate payment account.

6. Retention and deletion

Local media and records remain until you remove them or the operating system removes the app's data. Deleting only a record does not necessarily delete the original file; use the app's file-deletion option and check any copies you exported. You can clear browser history and WebKit cookies or caches in Settings.

Cookies copied into the system download store can persist separately until expiry or removal of app data. Clearing browser cookies does not guarantee that an existing download has been canceled or signed out. Cancel an unwanted transfer in Downloads.

Disable the privacy lock while unlocked to remove its Keychain verifier. Uninstalling removes the app container, but exported copies, backups and Keychain items can be subject to different Apple retention rules. We cannot remotely erase data stored only on your device or copies held by another service.

7. These pages and optional support

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading its pages, icon or demo files sends standard web-request information to GitHub's hosting infrastructure. These pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts and do not set application cookies. GitHub's privacy statement applies to its hosting services.

If you choose to contact support or open a public GitHub issue, the relevant service and the developer receive the information you choose to provide. It is used to answer your request. Public issues are visible to others; do not include passwords, private videos, purchase credentials or other sensitive information. Support providers retain correspondence according to their policies. You may request correction or removal through the same support channel.

8. Age suitability, updates and contact

This is a general-purpose browser and media tool, not a service designed for children. Follow the age rating and regional availability shown in the App Store. Only save content you own or are authorized by its source to download; the app does not grant third-party content rights.

We update the date on this page when the policy changes. For privacy questions, use the published support channels.
