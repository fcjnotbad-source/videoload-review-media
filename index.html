VideoLoad

Background download demonstration

A continuous recording of a user-started download and its foreground service notification.

Steps demonstrated

  1. Start a downloadTap the download button in the browser, then select the MP4 video.
  2. Check the download listThe app shows the active download and transferred bytes.
  3. Leave the appReturn to the Android home screen while the transfer continues.
  4. View the notificationOpen the notification shade to see the ongoing download and its progress.
  5. Return to the appTap the notification to return to the download list.
Open video directly (MP4)

Permission: FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC
Use case: user-initiated network downloads.

Recorded on Android 15 using the Play debug build, version 1.6.10-debug. The download service implementation is shared with the Play release.

Sample video: MDN's CC0 flower video example. Network speed was limited for the demonstration.
